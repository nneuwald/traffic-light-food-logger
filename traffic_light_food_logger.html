Settings & classification rules

Color rules follow Epstein's Traffic Light (Stoplight) Diet approach: green = low-calorie foods eaten freely (most fruits & vegetables), yellow = staple foods eaten in sensible portions, red = foods high in added sugar or fat, or sugary drinks, tracked against a weekly red-food budget. Thresholds above are editable so you can match your program's protocol. Every classification shows its reasons and can be overridden per item.

⚠️ Prototype for professional use alongside clinical judgment; not medical advice. Data lives in this open page only: use Backup (JSON) to save and Restore to reload. Nutrition data from USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts; branded records are label-deep, so verify against the package when accuracy matters.
