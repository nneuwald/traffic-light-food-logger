Settings & classification rules

Colours follow Epstein's Traffic Light Diet as set out in The Food & Activity Reference Guide (2012): each food group has a calorie range per serving and only vegetables can be green. Fruit juice, dried fruit and everything in the fats, oils and sweets group are always red; water, plain tea and coffee carry no colour. Cold cereal is also red when more than 25% of its calories come from sugar. The guide allows no more than 2 red servings a day. Serving sizes above are gram estimates of the guide's household servings and are editable. Every classification shows its reasons, the food group can be changed, and any colour can be overridden per item.

⚠️ Prototype for professional use alongside clinical judgment; not medical advice. Colours follow the Traffic Light Diet Food Reference Guide (Epstein, 2012); where a product is listed in the guide, the guide wins.
